I am Mohsen Niksirat, a software engineer based in Tabriz holding an M.Sc. in Computer Architecture. My work sits at the intersection of practical web engineering, multi-provider AI infrastructure, interactive educational software, and cross-engine systems programming.

Why This Publication Exists

Across shipping 47+ public repositories on GitHub (ranging from AI gateways like 9router and AIDahl to the FSRS-powered Readner suite, Repodest, MCP Studio, and the WebAssembly roguelite Horde Survival 3D), the most valuable engineering lessons always lived in the edge cases: rate-limit storms, browser memory ceilings, offline ACID storage, and bidirectional RTL typography. This publication documents those architectural trade-offs with real code, interactive sandboxes, and zero filler.

Engineering Principles

  • Local-first by default: Whenever a developer utility or data workbench can execute inside browser memory with zero uploads, eliminating the backend makes the tool faster, cheaper, and inherently private.
  • Design for upstream failure: AI gateways and bot runtimes should treat provider rate limits and timeouts as routine control flow, never fatal exceptions.
  • Bilingual craft: Every essay here is authored natively in both English and Persian RTL with self-hosted optical-size typography and verified WCAG AA contrast.
